# Operating guidelines (edit these to match your team's real practice)

Review HIGH priority PENDING requests before MEDIUM and LOW ones, and anything overdue before everything else.

An APPROVED request that has no trip should be consolidated promptly. If consolidation has run and a request is still unassigned, check whether its destination text differs slightly from similar requests or whether its time is more than 2 hours from them.

A trip loaded below about 30 percent of vehicle capacity is a candidate for review, for example whether a smaller vehicle would fit. A trip above about 90 percent leaves little room for added requests.

A PLANNED trip with no driver or no vehicle cannot be dispatched and should be fixed first. A trip that is ready but not dispatched close to its scheduled time needs attention.

Treat a claim for much more distance than the GPS trail shows as a signal to double check, and remember the rules engine already rejects claims beyond the 10 percent tolerance.
